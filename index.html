Since 1987

Nearly four decades of engineering trust.

Truman Electronics has led Egypt's electronics and satellite systems industry since 1987, pairing advanced German technology with decades of local expertise. What began with satellite receivers now spans televisions, air conditioners, household appliances and surveillance.

The company is built on job creation and contribution to the Egyptian economy — and on a forward-looking vision that leans into the Internet of Things and the next generation of connected home technology.
